ABOUT FINDRLIB

Research efficiently. Open the source.

FindrLib helps developers, students, researchers, and curious builders discover resources from several public indexes in one place. Search broadly, compare results, then use the original publisher's page for current instructions and terms.

Search indexes and page checks can miss resources or become stale. A reachable page confirms access at check time, not accuracy, safety, pricing, or reuse rights.

Local by design

Public search works without an account. A signed-in workspace stores your research in this browser; it is not synced to a server.

PRIVACY POLICY

Your research stays in this browser.

Guests can search without a saved personal workspace. Signed-in searches, bookmarks, and collections are stored in this browser's local storage under the verified Google account identifier; there is no user database or cloud sync. Logout clears the active session and on-screen account data while leaving that account's local workspace available for its next verified sign-in. Legacy guest data from earlier versions remains in browser storage but is no longer shown or updated.

Search queries are sent to the selected public providers to retrieve results. The local server keeps a crawl cache of checked public pages and provider health metrics without storing personal search history. Google processes sign-in under its own terms. Browser images and favicons may be requested from their publishers. On the hosted site, Google Auto ads may appear across pages and a clearly labeled AdSense placement may load on Discover. Google may process device identifiers, cookies, and ad interactions under its policies; regional consent choices are presented where required. Exported JSON files are saved wherever you choose. This local beta has no account-deletion service; clear this browser's FindrLib storage to remove local workspace data. Operator contact: Perugupalli Saai Abhishikth.
