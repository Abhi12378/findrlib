Privacy Policy

ResourceFinder is owned by Perugupalli Saai Abhishikth.

Search and saved data

Guests can search without a saved personal workspace. Search requests are processed by Netlify Functions and the public resource providers needed to return results. ResourceFinder does not keep a server-side user database or personal search history. Signed-in bookmarks, collections, and search history are saved in this browser's local storage under the verified Google account identifier. Signing out clears the current session and screen; local data can reappear when the same account signs in on this browser. You can clear this site's browser storage to remove it.

Third-party services

Google processes sign-in under its own policies. Search providers receive the query needed to find resources. Images and favicons may load from their publishers. Netlify processes hosting and function requests. The site may show a clearly labeled Google AdSense placement on the Discover page. Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers, and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads, subject to regional consent choices and Google's policies.

Your choices

For eligible visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, Google's consent message lets you accept, decline, or manage advertising choices. The site will provide a way to revisit those choices when the message is active. Browser settings can also restrict cookies and site storage. Ad blockers may prevent advertisements from loading.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the owner through the LinkedIn profile. The in-app support form drafts a message; it does not transmit feedback automatically.
